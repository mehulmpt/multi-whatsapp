      Storage

      — used by WhatsApp Web and saved accounts in this browser

      Good to know

      Switching saves the open WhatsApp session in your browser, then loads the other account's saved session and reloads WhatsApp. A switch usually takes a second or two. Accounts with a lot of history take longer.

      Use one WhatsApp tab. Other WhatsApp tabs are closed during a switch, because they would block it.

      Removing an account deletes its saved session from this browser only. To unlink it completely, also remove it on your phone under WhatsApp → Linked devices.

      Clearing site data or cookies for web.whatsapp.com deletes all saved sessions. You would then need to link each account again with its QR code.

      Everything stays on this device. The extension makes no network requests, has no analytics and never reads your messages.
